Method and limitations

The original global SAPS 3 model uses logit = −32.6659 + 7.3068 × ln(score + 20.5958), followed by p = 1 / (1 + e−logit). Its endpoint is hospital mortality, not ICU mortality.

SAPS 3 uses variables assessed at or within approximately one hour of ICU admission. Local case mix and geographic calibration affect accuracy. This tool does not validate score entry, select regional equations, or provide clinical decisions.
